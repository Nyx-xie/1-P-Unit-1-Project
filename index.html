Nana is a manga and anime written by author Ai Yazawa. Both the manga and anime are my all time favorite pieces of media. The story is about two girls both named Nana that meet on a train going to Tokyo. They eventually become roommates and best friends. Nana Komatsu is a bubbly boy crazy fashion obsessed girl and she quickly attaches herself to Nana Osaki. Nana Osaki is a singer in a punk band and has a very tough exterior.
